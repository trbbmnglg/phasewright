# Evidence

Read from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for the basis of any practice. Contents:
how to read the table, the method sources, the specify and measure sources,
the automation and AI sources, the import sources, the evidence on Design
Thinking itself, what was considered and not used.

Accessed 2026-10-30. A quote comes from a page cited in the same row. Where
an author, date or wording could not be confirmed on that page, the table says
so.

Strength labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Standard | An international or national standards body or institute |
| Peer-reviewed | A journal or conference paper |
| Method owner | Published by the people who created or run the method |
| Practitioner | A professional body, consultancy or practitioner publication |
| Vendor | Sold by, or commissioned by, a company with an interest |

"Adoption" gives what the source itself shows about use. "None found" means no
usage figure was found, not that the method is unused.
